Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy covers personal software tools built and operated solely by Justin Smith for his own use — including tools that connect to Google APIs (Gmail, Google Calendar, and Google Contacts) on his own Google account.

What this is

These are single-user personal automation tools, not a public product or service. No one other than Justin Smith uses them or has access to the data they touch.

Data accessed

Where a tool connects to a Google API, it accesses only the data needed for that tool's function on Justin Smith's own Google account, using an OAuth token he grants and controls directly. Access is read-only. The scopes used are:

  • gmail.readonly — message threads, to track outstanding replies and commitments
  • calendar.readonly — events, to keep a history of meetings and attendees
  • contacts.readonly — names and addresses, to resolve who appears in that history

No tool sends email, modifies calendars, or changes contacts.

Limited Use

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not used to develop, improve, or train generalized artificial intelligence models, and is not transferred to third parties except as required by law.

Data sharing

No data is sold, shared, published, or made available to any third party. Data is not used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the tool for its owner.

Data storage

Access tokens and any cached data are stored locally on Justin Smith's own devices and infrastructure. Nothing is transmitted to or stored on third-party servers beyond the Google APIs the tool directly calls.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Security → Third-party access.
